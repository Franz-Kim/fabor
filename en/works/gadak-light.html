Moiré Lamp : Gadak

A single stroke across the space.

Supported by the tension of a stainless-steel frame, thin 3D-printed fabric draws a curve. The density of its pattern changes along a continuous surface, revealing delicate moiré in the light.

The work considers the tension of a material stretching and bending alongside the passage of light. A precisely designed structure reads as a fluid line in space.

Profile

Seungki Kim

Designer · FABOR

Today’s technology,
at its most beautiful.

Seungki Kim is a designer connecting engineering precision with the aesthetics of handcraft. After earning a PhD in Industrial Design from KAIST, he worked as a senior designer at Samsung Electronics. His practice has since spanned a media-art duo, Hyundai Motor’s ZER01NE creator program and creative direction at an AI startup, leading to the founding of FABOR.

As an artist in residence at Sindang Creative Arcade, Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture, he continues to explore form through 3D printing. Through precisely designed patterns and thin, flexible surfaces, he brings the beauty of contemporary technology into lighting, objects and spaces.
